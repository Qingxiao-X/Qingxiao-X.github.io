Qingxiao (Kit) Xu

Ph.D. Student in Computer Science · Texas A&M University

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student at Texas A&M University, where I work in the O2 Lab with Prof. Jeff Huang. My research interests include program analysis, fuzzing, concurrent systems, and agentic and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems.

My current research focuses on building testing systems for real-world codebases, particularly techniques for detecting logical races and other race conditions in Linux kernel Rust drivers.

Before joining Texas A&M, I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Beijing Normal University in 2023, advised by Prof. Xianchuan Yu. I also conducted a research internship at Tsinghua University under the supervision of Prof. Fangwen Yu and Prof. Luping Shi.

I am always open to discussions and collaborations — feel free to reach out!
